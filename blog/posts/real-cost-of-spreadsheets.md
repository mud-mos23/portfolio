Every accounting department I've spoken to uses spreadsheets. Most admit it's a problem. The data backs them up.

## The Hidden Costs

A [KPMG study](https://home.kpmg/xx/en/home/insights/2021/03/the-hidden-cost-of-spreadsheets.html) found that 88% of spreadsheets contain errors. The average enterprise loses **$5-8 million annually** due to spreadsheet-related mistakes.

The breakdown:
- **Manual data entry errors**: miss keyed numbers, wrong formulas (60% of errors)
- **Version control chaos**: 15 copies of the same "final" budget
- **Audit trail? What audit trail?**: No record of who changed what or when

## The Spreadsheet Trap

Spreadsheets are flexible. That's their strength and their curse. They let companies skip the hard work of designing proper processes.

## The Fix

Not an ERP for everything — but structured tools for structured tasks:
- Budgeting → dedicated module with approval workflows
- Reconciliation → automated matching rules
- Reporting → parameterized report templates

The ERP modules I built in [FraudShield](https://github.com/mud-mos23/fraud-detection) and [Bubely](https://github.com/mud-mos23/bubely-erp) target these specific pain points.

Spreadsheets aren't going away. But they should be the exception, not the infrastructure.
